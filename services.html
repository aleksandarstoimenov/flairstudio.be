Questions

What do we need to provide to start a project?

Plans, sections and elevations, a 3D model if you have one, material references and a few images that show the mood you're after. Whatever stage you're at is fine: we can also start from a sketch.

What file formats do you work with?

Most often, FBX for 3D models, and CAD (DWG) or PDF for plans. If you work in another format, send what you have and we'll tell you.

Do you work with both architects and developers?

Yes. Most of our work is with architecture studios, for design, competitions and permits. For developers, we make the images and films that sell a project, often a full set of interiors, and carry them into brochures, websites and social media.

How do you keep us involved along the way?

We share each step before moving on: first the camera angles in simple renders, then a draft with light and materials, then the final images. You comment at every stage. And if the project evolves later, we update the images with you.

Can you help with competition submissions?

Yes, it's a big part of what we do. We help you choose the few views that carry the idea of the project best, and deliver them ready for the boards. Schweitzer, in Anderlecht, is a recent example: the competition was won with our photo insertion.

How much notice do you need for a competition?

The earlier the better, so we can keep time free for you. Get in touch as soon as you know you're entering: a rough brief is enough to book the slot, and the details can follow.

Which kind of image is right for our project?

It depends on who you need to convince. A jury needs a strong idea in a few images, a permit file needs your project in its real context, and buyers need to picture themselves living there. Tell us the goal and we'll suggest the views, and whether an image or a film fits best.

How much do renders or films cost?

Every project is different: the number of views, interior or exterior, the level of detail and the deadline all play a part. Send us your plans and we'll come back with a clear quote.

Can you send a quote before we start?

Always. You get a quote with the views, the steps and the delivery date before anything starts, and we only begin once you've agreed to it.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the number of images and how far along the design is. We agree on a delivery date together at the start, and we keep it. If you're in a rush, let us know and we'll tell you straight away what's possible.
